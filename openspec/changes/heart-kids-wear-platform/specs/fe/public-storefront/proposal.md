# Proposal: Public Storefront Pages (FE)

## Why
Customers must easily discover products through brand group buying campaigns and two-tier category filters, view expandable size charts, register with complete 7-11 and survey data, confirm pre-order commitments via modal dialogs, and check out with automated discounts and travel date notes.

## What Changes
- `HomePage` with hero campaign banners, category cards, and product grids.
- `ProductListPage` with two-tier category pill filters and price sorting.
- `ProductDetailPage` with image carousel, size charts, SKU variant selector, and "滿4,000折60元" reminder.
- `LoginPage` (case-insensitive email, password visibility eye toggle, remember me).
- `RegisterPage` (7-11 store lookup link to `emap.pcsc.com.tw`, marketing survey, mandatory shopping rules checkbox modal).
- `CartPage` with item previews and mandatory pre-order verification modal.
- `CheckoutPage` with saved address dropdown, 15-item shipping lock, discount summary, Terms & Conditions toggle, bank account details, and post-payment travel modal.

## Impact
- Core customer-facing shopping experience.
